How to play

Move / drive: WASD, SHIFT to run, SPACE handbrake in a car.
Steal a car: walk up to any car and press E. Parked cars are free; driven ones raise your wanted level.
Combat: click to shoot, 1/2/3 switch weapons, F to punch.
Crew: Q uses your lead's ability — Dario's Focus slows time, Carmen's Nitro boosts any car. C switches lead.
Money: missions (M), street races (R), taxi fares (T) and 20 hidden golden packages ($250 each).
Wanted: 1–5 stars. Cruisers chase and PIT you, roadblocks appear at 3 stars, a helicopter at 4. Break line of sight to cool off.
Standing: each district tracks how much it respects you — work a district to open it up.
